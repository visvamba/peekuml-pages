Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

In short: PeekUML does not collect, store or share any data about you. Your diagrams are rendered on your Mac and are never sent anywhere.

What PeekUML does with your files

PeekUML reads a PlantUML file only when you preview it in Finder, open it in the app, or drag it onto the app window. It turns the file into an image on your Mac and shows you the result. PeekUML doesn't upload, copy, index or keep your files. It doesn't keep the rendered images either, unless you choose Copy, which puts the image on your clipboard.

PeekUML runs in Apple's App Sandbox and does not have permission to use the network. The part of the app that renders diagrams also blocks PlantUML's !include feature, so a diagram can't make PeekUML read other files or load anything from the internet.

Data we collect

None. PeekUML does not include analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and it doesn't contain any third-party software development kits (SDKs). It doesn't need an account, and it never asks for your personal information.

Information handled by Apple

Apple processes your App Store purchase under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your payment details. If you turned on Share with App Developers in macOS, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We use them only to fix problems in PeekUML. You can change this setting in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

If you contact us

If you report a problem on GitHub, your message and any files you attach are public and follow GitHub's privacy statement. We use what you send only to answer your question or fix the problem.

Children

PeekUML collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If PeekUML ever changes how it handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the new version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub Issues.
